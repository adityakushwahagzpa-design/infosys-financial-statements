# Infosys – Financial Statements & Ratio Analysis (FY2026)

Infosys Limited's consolidated financial statements for **FY2025 and FY2026** rebuilt in Excel (₹ crore), with ratio, common-size and integrity checks.

## Build
- **Data** sheet: every reported line item entered once
- **P&L, balance sheet and cash flow** built entirely with formulas (INDEX/MATCH, SUMIFS)
- **Ratio analysis**: growth, profitability, returns, liquidity, efficiency, leverage, cash flow and payout
- **Common-size** and year-on-year analysis
- **Checks**: balance sheet balances, cash ties to the balance sheet, profit agrees across statements

## Key findings (FY2026)
- Revenue up **9.6%**, net profit up **10.2%**; net margin **16.5%**
- ROE **31.2%** (from 27.9%); operating margin 20.9%
- Free cash flow **₹31,259 crore**, 106% of net profit
- Payout (dividends + buyback) at **117% of free cash flow**; no borrowings
